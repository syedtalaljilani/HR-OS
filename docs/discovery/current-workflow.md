# Current Recruitment Workflow

## Existing Process

```text
Job Opening
    ↓
CVs Received
    ↓
Open CV
    ↓
Read CV
    ↓
Manual Excel Data Entry
    ↓
Candidate Screening
    ↓
Interview Call
    ↓
Track Call / Response in Excel
    ↓
Interview Attendance Tracking
    ↓
Department Interview
    ↓
Paper Interview Feedback
    ↓
Manual Score / Feedback Review
    ↓
HR Evaluation
    ↓
Paper Based HR Feedback
    ↓
Candidate Comparison
    ↓
Final HR Decision
    ↓
Candidate Communication
```

## Step Breakdown

### 1. Job Opening

HR opens a position and starts receiving applications.

### 2. CV Collection

Candidates submit their CVs.

### 3. CV Review

HR manually opens and reads CVs.

### 4. Candidate Data Entry

Relevant candidate information is manually entered into Excel.

Typical information includes:

```text
Name
Address
Degree
Other candidate details
```

### 5. Screening

HR reviews candidate information and determines which candidates appear suitable for the position.

### 6. Candidate Calling

Suitable candidates are contacted by phone for interview coordination.

### 7. Call Tracking

Candidate response and call information are manually tracked in Excel.

### 8. Interview Attendance

Interview attendance is also tracked manually.

### 9. Department Interview

Candidates attend the department interview.

Interviewers provide feedback using paper based evaluation.

### 10. HR Interview

Candidates proceed to the HR interview.

HR evaluation is also recorded manually on paper.

### 11. Candidate Comparison

HR reviews candidate information and interview feedback to compare candidates.

### 12. Final Decision

HR makes the final hiring decision.

### 13. Candidate Communication

Candidates are informed about the outcome.

## Main Manual Handoffs

```text
CV → HR
HR → Excel
Excel → Calling
Calling → Excel
Interview → Paper
Paper → HR Review
HR Review → Final Decision
```

These handoffs are the main areas targeted by the Recruitment OS.
