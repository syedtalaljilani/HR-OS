# Stakeholder Notes

## Stakeholder

**Name:** Luqman
**Role:** Team Lead HR
**Organization:** Evyol Group of Industries

## Discussion Objective

Understand the current recruitment workflow, identify repetitive manual work, and define the operational problems that the HR Recruitment OS should address.

## Current Recruitment Process

HR receives CVs for open positions. Jobs may receive a large number of applications from candidates across Pakistan.

The current process mainly depends on Excel, manual CV review, phone calls, and paper based interview evaluations.

## Current Tools

| Activity                        | Current Method |
| ------------------------------- | -------------- |
| Candidate information           | Excel          |
| CV review                       | Manual         |
| Candidate screening             | Manual         |
| Candidate calling               | Phone          |
| Call/response tracking          | Excel          |
| Interview attendance            | Excel          |
| Department interview evaluation | Paper          |
| HR interview evaluation         | Paper          |
| Candidate comparison            | Manual         |
| Final decision                  | HR             |

## Key Observations

HR manually opens CVs and extracts candidate information.

Candidate details such as name, address, degree and other information are entered into Excel manually.

Suitable candidates are contacted for interviews.

Call responses and interview attendance are tracked manually.

Department interviews use paper based evaluation forms.

HR interviews also use paper based evaluation.

HR then reviews the collected information and makes the final hiring decision.

## Main Problems Identified

1. Repetitive candidate data entry
2. Manual CV screening
3. Excel based candidate tracking
4. Paper based interview evaluations
5. Manual comparison of candidates
6. Interview coordination and tracking effort
7. Candidate information spread across different manual records

## Desired Outcome

Create a paperless and structured recruitment workflow where candidate information, screening, interviews, evaluations, communication and status tracking are handled through one system.

## Human Decision Making

AI should assist HR with candidate information extraction, validation, matching and recommendations.

HR remains responsible for reviewing candidates and making the final hiring decision.

## Important Constraint

No automatic hiring decision should be made by the system.

**Principle:**

> AI recommends. Human verifies. HR decides.
